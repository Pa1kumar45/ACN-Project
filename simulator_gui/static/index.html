Mathematical Scaling Formulations

ScaleLB Proactive Threshold (Eq. 1)
$$\frac{B}{A \times \alpha} \ge \gamma$$

Adds an AMF when gNB load ratio crosses threshold γ. Suffers 40s cold-start spike during sudden surges.

ScaleLB Reactive Threshold (Eq. 2)
$$\frac{B}{A \times \alpha} > 1.0$$

Scales only after AMF capacity is completely exhausted. Severe latency degradation and packet drops.

Our LSTM Predictive Scaling Rule (Eq. 4)
$$N(t) = \left\lceil \frac{\hat{\lambda}(t + H)(1 + m)}{\mu \gamma} \right\rceil$$

Forecasts arrival rate λ̂ at lookahead horizon H ≥ Tboot + Δ (45–60s) with safety margin m. Boots AMFs early to completely eliminate cold-start lag!

Hysteresis & Graceful Draining
$$\hat{\lambda}(t + H) < 0.40 \times \mu \times A \quad (\text{sustained } 45\text{s})$$

Scale-in transitions AMF to DRAINING state: accepts no new sessions, terminates only after active sessions complete.
